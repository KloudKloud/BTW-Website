Between Two Worlds

Do you believe in magic?

When an unexpected kidnapping flips the region on its head, the pokémon of Sylva Skies must band together to unravel the science of the unknown. Every clue reveals another mystery, and every puzzle draws them further from home.

Will they save their world in time? Or discover a new one along the way?

Follow Kloud, a go-lucky Umbreon with a knack for trouble; Saphero, an Espeon with both paws grappled in science and feelings he can't comprehend; and Inferno, the Blaziken detective who weaves every piece left behind, one tragedy at a time.

In a tale of love, adventure, mystery, and fate, follow our group of heroes as they embrace the secrets of their universe.

About the Author

💬 Join the Discord Server

Thanks for visiting my page! If you're here, that means you're pretty cool. Very cool even. <3

Between Two Worlds is a story about fun, adventure, and adult story-telling in a pokemon-themed world. I started this story as a way to alleviate the stress from college/Software Engineering, and since its inception, I've been captivated by it.

Beyond making sparky characters and goofing around, I dedicate a lot of time into personal, side projects, both creative and for school. I'm a developer of several writing-related Discord bots, fanfic parsers, dappled with music (STILL LEARNING EJKGBERJKG), and enjoy competitive pokemon whenever I'm not writing.

Currently, I graduate Spring of 2027, and I am double majoring in Computer Science and Math. I love STEM and gay, doomed yaoi, so I'd say I have the best of both worlds~

If you'd like to see my other projects, as well as join the community Discord server (18+ only), visit the links on the right! If you'd like to browse chapters and leave personal comments for me, you can do so via the Inbox tab after making a free account, and I'll try and respond as quick as I can! Donations are all optional and totally your choice, but if you'd like to support my other projects/me personally, all donations are greatly appreciated and will be displayed on this website! <3 (But of course, no pressure~)

To make an account and access spicy artwork and one-shots, you must be 18+. This story is not suitable for anyone below that age. Thanks so much for your understanding!
