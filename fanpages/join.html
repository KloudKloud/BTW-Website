Create A Page For Your Story!

Are you a writer who wants to have a page, similar to this one, for your own story? A place to share your characters, art, and chapters with the BTW community?

You're in luck!

As a passion project for my supporters, I've designed a method for all of you to host your own books right here in our community, and show off your world/lore! If you have an audience of your own, you'll be able to send them a link to your specific page, and keep your audience in-the-know for your story.

This is completely free, and an extension of my gratitude for supporting me/my story! All I ask is that you treat one another with respect, and maybe recommend Between Two Worlds to a friend down the line.
