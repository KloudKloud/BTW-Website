Ratings & Tags

Every gallery submission is labeled with one of three ratings. The rating you pick controls who can see the piece and where it shows up around the site.

General — Safe for work. Visible to everyone, logged in or not, regardless of NSFW settings.
Mature — Suggestive or thematically mature content that isn't explicit. Requires an account with NSFW content enabled in Account Settings to view.
Explicit — Explicit 18+ content. Requires an account with NSFW content enabled in Account Settings to view.

Labeling your work

All NSFW (18+) artwork must be labeled as Mature or Explicit — never General. Mislabeling NSFW content as General to get more visibility is not allowed and may result in the post being relabeled or removed.

Tags

Tags help people find (and avoid) the content they're looking for. Keep tags accurate and relevant to what's actually in the piece.
