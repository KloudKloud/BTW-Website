Content & Agreement

Between Two Worlds is dedicated to hosting transformative, creative, and non-commercial content created by fans from all over the world.

There are several modes of "content" that exist on our platform, highlighted below.

1. Stories (Fanfiction, Originals, etc.)

Written works, published through our custom editor.

  • Stories should be properly rated, following our content rating.
  • All works must be original. Unless given explicit permission, Between Two Worlds is not a site for posting copyrighted stories that belong to someone else.

Plagiarism

Plagiarism is the use of someone else's words, or their expressions of their ideas, without attribution. Simply altering the names or rearranging words does not make a work your own.

AI

Between Two Worlds supports authentic, user-made content exclusively. AI writing and stories are not to be published here.

2. Artwork / Gallery / Characters

Between Two Worlds is dedicated to sharing all types of content, including characters, artwork, and general posts.

  • Artwork must follow appropriate ratings, as indicated in our content guidelines.

Character References

  • Characters are meant to be fun, expressive cards that advertise lore, stories, and details surrounding your universe.
  • Art used for character refs doesn't have to be original/commissioned. All we ask is that, when possible, you reference the source you pulled the art from.
  • This does not include art locked behind a paywall/explicitly barred from public use. Exclusive Patreon content or other locked artwork should not be published as character refs onto BTW, for any reason.

Gallery Posts

  • Pictures posted onto your gallery section should be strictly art that you own, produce, or have explicit permission to post.
  • You can make descriptions, tag characters, tag stories, and customize your gallery posts to your liking. These serve as a way to show off artwork and share lore with your community. Post sketches, commissions, drawings, or anything you like, and attach stories or wacky descriptions that tell the narrative behind them.
  • Make sure gallery posts are rated correctly.

3. Newspapers

Newspapers are conversation boards/journals posted onto your page to update your community. Followers and viewers of your profile will be notified whenever you post.

4. Clubs

Clubs is a social page dedicated to joining groups and discovering niches that you enjoy. Clubs include members, social hubs, admins, and more.

What is a Club?

  • Clubs are classified as groups of people with a shared interest. Anyone can start a club about a topic they enjoy: a gaming group, writing group, PMD art group, a story fan club, etc.
  • Clubs have a designated owner. Owners can assign admins and moderate the posts members make within the club. Owners also create rules, make additional pages, and decide what kind of community they wish to cater.

Club Posts

  • Every member of a club can create a post. Admins/Owners can make posts in a special section, documented as the "Moderator News Feed". Posts involve an image preview, a title, and the body text of what discussion is being brought up.
  • E.g. A story fan club, where the author of a story begins a community for their fic. Members may post discussions about chapters, or upload fanart/reactions to certain scenes that stood out.
  • More details about posts and what to expect in the Clubs tab at the top.

FAQ

"Is this website 18+?"

Yes. All users must pass an age-gate before accessing this site, and we limit account creation and posting to 18+ only.

"Can I make a club that discusses adult content?"

Yes, absolutely. Whether that be sexual discussions relating to books or life, adult discussions are fine in the club sections, so long as you mark your club as NSFW. We only limit the posting of IRL nudity or sexual pictures. General discussions about sex and adult topics are fine.

"Is this site purely for fanfiction and fandom-related art only?"

No. Between Two Worlds is open to the broader nerd, furry, and niche "fantasy" audience. We encourage creators of all kinds to dip their fingers into our website, and we're working hard to add features that people from every niche can enjoy.

Ratings & Tags

Content uploaded onto the website falls under three categories:

General — Safe for all audiences.
Mature — Adult themes (sex, violence, etc.) that aren't as graphic as explicit-rated content, but not suitable for all audiences.
Explicit — Explicit adult themes, such as porn, graphic violence, etc.

All stories and gallery submissions to the website will display their rating directly. Users must confirm that they are 18 or older to access any content on the website, and for that reason, no content is hidden by default.

Users who wish to turn off mature and explicit content can navigate to their Account Settings and enable SFW browsing mode.

There are also filtering options for every search a user makes, with ratings as a toggleable menu.

Tags

What is a tag?

A tag is a searchable keyword or phrase that provides information about a work. They allow content creators to identify their submissions and specify which Ratings, Categories, Characters, Fandoms, and Relationships apply. Furthermore, Additional Tags allow for specific keywords/phrases that boost discoverability.

How We Organize Them

We have a growing, permanent tag dictionary that eases the stress of inventing new tags every time. Popular fandoms, phrases, kinks, keywords, descriptions, etc. are added to our dictionary, and make finding the right tags a breeze.

Similar to AO3, we wrangle tags into distinct categories automatically. "Blacky | Umbreon" will be wrangled into a more popular, universal "Umbreon" tag on the website. Longer phrases and keywords that can be said in numerous ways but mean the same thing will be manually wrangled to maximize discoverability (e.g., "MLP Community → My Little Pony").

Tags are still expanding! We're constantly updating our dictionary, and there is a good chance the tags you enter may not appear automatically in our dictionary in the early months of our launch. That's why we rely on you all, as a community, to grow and work together to broaden our scope.

Groups of Tags

Categories

Category tags indicate what kind of romantic and/or sexual relationships are depicted in a work. These are primarily relevant for stories.

  • Gen — No romantic/sexual relationships, or not the main focus
  • M/F — Male/Female relationships
  • M/M — Male/Male relationships
  • F/F — Female/Female relationships
  • Multi — Multiple relationships, or a relationship with more than one partner
  • Other — Other relationships

Fandom

This, too, applies mainly to story-search algorithms. Fandoms allow you to specify the source material or "community" your work belongs to.

You can view our list of fandoms here.

Relationships

Relationship tags indicate which characters are involved in romantic or platonic relationships in the work. We generally list the full names of the characters, separated with a "/". (Ex: "Umbreon/Espeon, Ash Ketchum/Sarena, Rainbow Dash/Twilight Sparkle")

NOTES! Like Additional Tags, we utilize a tag dictionary for every group above. New fandoms and relationships are being added every day, and if you have a unique item to add, it will be wrangled in for the future.
