Relationships
Tag the relationships in your work. This applies heavily for fanfiction, and should be used to group recognizable characters together (e.g. "Umbreon"/"Espeon") to help discoverability. If the characters don't appear as a selectable object, enter them with their full names, and use a "/" to denote individual characters. Please be advised, if your relationship includes several characters, consider shortening their names if needed to ensure that the tag stays under 150 characters!

Fandom
Start typing and pick a match if one shows up! That keeps everyone's tagging consistent so stories in the same fandom can actually find each other. Don't see yours? Type it anyway; we'll add popular new ones to the official list over time.
Additional Tags
Tag up to 100 simple, relevant, searchable tags to help your story be discovered! For example: Pokemon, Furry, Mystery, Love, Ships, Umbreon, Lucario, Sexual Intimacy... Enter tags one at a time. Duplicate tags will be auto-deleted for your convienency. No need to tag specific character names: your character names are tied inside of your story and will be searchable in meta-data by default. Still tag species or other relevant traits about them that you feel could attract readers.
