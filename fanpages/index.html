Meet VeekitPaws

Literally an Eevee IRL~ ^w^

Hiya! You can call me Vee, Veekit, Eevee, or anything that floats your boat! I spend a bit too much time developing websites for nerdy, silly peeps like ourselves! :3 I hope you enjoy this little space, and I hope it helps in promoting your stories and centralizing your community!

I started this website after I realized how terrible Wattpad/AO3 was when it came to sharing the art/deeper lore of my book, Between Two Worlds. I wanted readers to be able to actually see my characters, not just read about them, and having a gallery/dedicated place to share all of my art, lore, passion, etc. compelled me to make this.

And I want to share that joy with all writers/creative peeps!

I have yet to see a great social, furry/pokemon writing site. My goal is to create it. I'd LOVE your feedback, advice, input, and thoughts if you have any ^w^ There is so much to do and explore, and I actively update and plot new features daily.

If you have any questions, please shoot me a message. I love you all, and thanks so much for stopping by!

🐾 VeekitPaws
